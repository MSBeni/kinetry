# Code of conduct

Kinetry follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at CONDUCT_CONTACT_EMAIL. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who doesn't follow this code.
